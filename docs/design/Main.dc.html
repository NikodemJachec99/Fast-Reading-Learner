Fast Reading · Design system v1.0

Read faster.
Understand more.
Remember longer.

One system for the whole app: brand foundations, Liquid Glass chrome, motion, every screen and all eight exercises. Paper stays calm for reading. Glass is for navigation and controls only.

Foundations Controls Motion Topology 10 screens 9 exercise screens
01
Text comes first

Reading surfaces are opaque paper in Literata. Nothing animates over a text while it is being read.

02
Glass for chrome

Tab bar, top controls, segmented controls and floating actions use Liquid Glass: blur, light rim, soft depth.

03
Motion with meaning

Springs confirm a tap, a right answer or a finished round. Every animation has a reduced motion fallback.

THE RULE
Speed counts only when you understood

Below 2/3 correct the WPM is shown, marked and left out of statistics.
