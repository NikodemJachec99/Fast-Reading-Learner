THE NEW LIBRARY

In 1998 Brena opened its first library in an old warehouse. Before, the nearest one was forty minutes away by bus, and most families owned only a few books. Within a year, loans nearly doubled. The librarian, Ewa Marek, started an evening club so people could talk about the novels they read. When the school ran out of space, it began holding lessons in the library.
